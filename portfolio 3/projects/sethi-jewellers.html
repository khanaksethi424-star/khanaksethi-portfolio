UI/UX

Sethi Jewellers

An intuitive interface for online jewellery shopping — designed to feel as trustworthy, personal, and aspirational as walking into the store.

Overview

Sethi Jewellers wanted an online shopping experience that didn't feel like a downgrade from an in-store visit. Jewellery is an emotional, high-trust purchase — and most e-commerce jewellery experiences are generic, impersonal, and don't earn that trust.

The problem

Online jewellery shopping is a hard sell: limited product visualization, confusing navigation and checkout, and no premium feel that matches the value of the product. Buyers can't easily tell if a piece is right for them without physically seeing and trying it on.

Process

Discover

I researched the market and competitors, and used Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs to map the emotional motivations behind jewellery purchases — safety, belonging, esteem, and self-actualization all show up differently depending on why someone is buying.

Empathize

I built personas and an empathy map, including a "Gift Buyer" persona who feels overwhelmed by options, values timely delivery, and wants a meaningful piece within budget — plus a full user journey map.

Define

The core problem: users want a luxury shopping experience online that feels as trustworthy, personalized, and aspirational as an in-store visit. I framed this into "how might we" questions around exploration, trust, virtual try-on, and personalization.

Ideate

I brainstormed features including AR virtual try-on, smart filters, wishlist and sharing, authenticity seals, and customization/engraving — then mapped the core user flow from homepage through search, product detail, try-on, wishlist, cart, checkout, payment, and confirmation.

Prototype

I moved from low-fidelity wireframes to a high-fidelity UI, using gold and black for a luxury feel, beige and white for minimalism, and a serif paired with a clean sans-serif for elegance without sacrificing readability.

Solution

The final UI covers the full shopping journey — homepage, product detail, wishlist, cart, and checkout — built around a visual language that signals trust and craftsmanship at every step.
