PECmate

"One campus, one ride." A peer-to-peer carpooling app exclusive to Punjab Engineering College students, letting them find or offer rides within a trusted, verified campus community.

RoleSolo — UI & UX
Duration[Add duration]
ToolsFigma
TeamSolo

Overview

PECmate is a self-initiated concept exploring campus-specific carpooling — designed end-to-end solo, from initial idea through to a working Figma prototype.

The problem

College students commuting to and from campus often rely on expensive individual transport, or unreliable, unsafe ad-hoc carpooling with strangers, with no real way to verify who they'd actually be riding with.

The solution

PECmate restricts sign-in to verified @pec.edu.in college email addresses, so every user is guaranteed to be a fellow student — removing the core trust problem of open ridesharing apps entirely through onboarding, rather than a heavier verification system. From the home screen, users choose to either find a ride or offer one as a driver. Ride listings show the driver's photo, name, rating, exact pickup and drop-off points, vehicle, available seats, and per-seat price, giving riders enough to decide and request a ride within two or three taps.

A few decisions worth calling out

Restricting the sign-up domain is a small detail with an outsized effect — it's the entire safety mechanism of the app, and it's achieved through onboarding rather than a complex verification flow. Rating, photo, and route pins on each ride card front-load exactly the information a rider needs, without extra taps. And the "find a ride" form stays to two fields plus a date and seat count, keeping friction low for a task students would repeat often.

Reflection

This was a self-initiated concept rather than a tested or shipped product — but it's the project that most shows a complete solo process, from identifying the problem through to a working, prototyped solution in Figma, without any team to lean on.
