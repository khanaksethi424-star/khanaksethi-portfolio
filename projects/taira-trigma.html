UI/UX · 4-month internship

Taira, for Trigma AI

A conversational AI assistant for Trigma's website that qualifies leads and books meetings through natural conversation, designed for three different people across the funnel — not just the visitor.

RoleUI/UX Design Intern
Duration4 months
ToolsFigma
TeamTrigma's design & marketing team

Overview

Taira is Trigma's AI-powered assistant, built to replace generic chatbots and high-friction contact forms with a natural, intent-aware conversation — offering visitors a choice between an AI video avatar or text chat, then qualifying them and booking meetings without ever feeling like a form.

The problem

B2B visitors landing on Trigma's site had no fast, low-friction way to evaluate fit. Contact forms ask for information before offering any value, and generic chatbots give scripted, irrelevant answers that don't adapt to where a visitor actually is in the buying journey.

Process

Discover & Empathize

Rather than researching a single user, I mapped three personas spanning the entire funnel — Arjun, a Senior PM evaluating fit and wanting instant, relevant answers with zero sales pressure; Manisha, an SDR who receives the AI-qualified lead and needs rich context to avoid cold, low-quality handoffs; and Neha, a RevOps Manager who needs clean, automatically-synced data across CRM, calendar, and communication tools. That reframed the brief from "design a chatbot" into "design a system that serves three different people across a pipeline."

Define

Distilling across all three personas, the core needs were: instant intelligence, natural rather than robotic conversation, low-friction and gradual information capture, multi-language support, and clean structured handoff data for the humans downstream.

Ideate

I mapped two full conversation flows. The text chat flow analyzes intent — low, medium, or high — and branches the conversation accordingly, only "soft-asking" for an email once enough value has already been exchanged, with graceful retry logic if declined. The AI avatar flow adds an explicit recording-consent step, natural Q&A to gather context like location, industry, and requirement, and a human-handoff path that only triggers during working hours, with its own separate consent step.

Prototype

Before any screens, I built a small design system — a color palette anchored on Trigma's signature red, a Poppins type scale from Display down to Small, button states, and reusable chat bubble components — then moved through grayscale wireframes to lock structure, before final high-fidelity screens matching Trigma's existing dark, premium brand identity.

Solution

A widget offering a choice between video chat with the AI avatar "Taira" or text chat. The assistant asks intent-clarifying questions, offers quick-reply chips for common paths, captures contact info progressively rather than upfront, and closes with direct meeting-slot selection and automatic calendar confirmation.

Outcome & reflection

The design wasn't deployed live by the end of the internship, but both conversation flows — text chat and AI avatar — were validated with the Trigma team, confirming the intent-based branching logic and progressive information capture approach ahead of handoff to development.
