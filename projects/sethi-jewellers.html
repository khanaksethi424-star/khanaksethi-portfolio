Sethi Jewellers

An intuitive interface for online jewellery shopping — designed to feel as trustworthy, personal, and aspirational as walking into the store.

Overview

Sethi Jewellers wanted an online shopping experience that didn't feel like a downgrade from an in-store visit. Jewellery is an emotional, high-trust purchase — and most e-commerce jewellery experiences are generic, impersonal, and don't earn that trust.

The problem

Online jewellery shopping is a hard sell: limited product visualization, confusing navigation and checkout, and no premium feel that matches the value of the product. Buyers can't easily tell if a piece is right for them without physically seeing and trying it on.

Process

Why people actually buy jewellery

I used Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs to map the emotional motivations behind a jewellery purchase — safety, belonging, esteem, and self-actualization show up very differently depending on whether someone's buying a gift, an investment, or something for themselves. That shaped everything downstream, including a "Gift Buyer" persona who feels overwhelmed by options, values timely delivery, and wants something meaningful within budget.

Where online jewellery shopping loses trust

Competitor research and a full user journey map made the real problem clear: people want a luxury experience online that feels as trustworthy, personalized, and aspirational as an in-store visit — and most sites don't come close. I framed this into "how might we" questions around exploration, trust, virtual try-on, and personalization.

Turning research into a flow

From there I brainstormed features — AR virtual try-on, smart filters, wishlist and sharing, authenticity seals, customization and engraving — and mapped the core user flow end to end, from homepage through search, product detail, try-on, wishlist, cart, checkout, payment, and confirmation.

A visual language for luxury

I moved from low-fidelity wireframes to a high-fidelity UI built on gold and black for luxury, beige and white for restraint, and a serif paired with a clean sans-serif — elegant without sacrificing readability.

Solution

The final UI covers the full shopping journey — homepage, product detail, wishlist, cart, and checkout — built around a visual language that signals trust and craftsmanship at every step.
